Chicken Tikka Masala

Quick Visual

Ingredients (Serves 3–4)

  • 500g chicken breast or thigh
  • Yogurt & lemon juice for marinade
  • Garam masala, cumin, coriander
  • Tomato puree
  • Butter & cream
  • Ginger & garlic paste
  • Salt & freshly cracked pepper

Method

  1. Marinate chicken in yogurt, spices, and lemon juice for at least 1 hour.
  2. Grill or pan-fry chicken until cooked through.
  3. Prepare tomato-butter sauce with spices and ginger-garlic paste.
  4. Add cooked chicken to sauce and simmer briefly.
  5. Finish with cream and garnish with fresh coriander.
  6. Serve hot with naan or rice.

Rich, creamy, and perfectly spiced.
