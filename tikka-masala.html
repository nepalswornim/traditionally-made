Chicken Tikka Masala

Quick Visual

Ingredients (Serves 3–4)

  • 500g chicken breast or thigh, cubed
  • 150g plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 400g tomato puree
  • 100ml cream
  • 2 tbsp butter or ghee
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 inch ginger, grated
  • Fresh coriander (cilantro) for garnish

Method

  1. Marinate chicken in yogurt, 1 tbsp garam masala, turmeric, and chili powder for 1–2 hours.
  2. Sauté onion, garlic, and ginger in butter until soft.
  3. Add tomato puree and remaining spices; simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Cook marinated chicken in the sauce until tender and fully cooked.
  5. Stir in cream, adjust seasoning with salt and pepper.
  6. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot with rice or naan.

Rich, creamy, and bursting with flavor. True Indian classic.
