Guanciale Carbonara

```

Quick Visual

Ingredients (Serves 2)

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 100g guanciale
  • 2 large egg yolks + 1 whole egg
  • 50g Pecorino Romano (finely grated)
  • Freshly cracked black pepper
  • Salt (for pasta water)

Method

  1. Bring a pot of salted water to boil and cook spaghetti until al dente.
  2. Cut guanciale into strips and render slowly in a cold pan until crisp.
  3. Whisk egg yolks, whole egg, pecorino, and lots of black pepper.
  4. Reserve pasta water. Add pasta to guanciale pan (off heat).
  5. Stir in egg mixture, loosening with pasta water until creamy.
  6. Serve immediately with extra pecorino and pepper.

No cream. No garlic. No shortcuts.
```
