AUTHENTIC RECIPE

Guanciale Carbonara

Rome. Eggs. Pecorino. Guanciale. Nothing else.

This is the **traditional Roman carbonara**, made the way it has always been. No cream. No bacon. No garlic. No shortcuts.

Ingredients (Serves 2)

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 100g guanciale (cured pork cheek)
  • 2 large egg yolks + 1 whole egg
  • 60g Pecorino Romano (finely grated)
  • Freshly cracked black pepper
  • Salt (for pasta water only)

Method

1. Render the guanciale

Cut guanciale into thick batons. Cook gently in a cold pan until the fat renders and the meat becomes crisp. Turn off heat and set aside.

2. Cook the pasta

Boil spaghetti in lightly salted water until al dente. Reserve one cup of pasta water.

3. Make the egg mixture

Whisk egg yolks, whole egg, Pecorino Romano, and plenty of black pepper until smooth.

4. Combine off heat

Add hot pasta to the guanciale pan. Remove from heat. Stir in egg mixture, loosening with pasta water until glossy and creamy.

5. Serve immediately

Finish with extra Pecorino and black pepper. Serve hot.

What Is NOT Authentic

  • ❌ Cream
  • ❌ Bacon or pancetta
  • ❌ Garlic or onion
  • ❌ Parmesan

If it has cream, it is not carbonara.
