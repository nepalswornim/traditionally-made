Butter Chicken

Quick Visual

Ingredients (Serves 3–4)

  • 500g chicken thighs
  • 50g butter
  • 200g tomato puree
  • 1 tbsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi
  • 100ml cream
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Marinate chicken with ginger-garlic paste and spices, then grill or sear until cooked.
  2. In a pan, melt butter and add tomato puree; simmer to thicken.
  3. Add cream and garam masala, stirring gently.
  4. Add chicken and coat in the sauce.
  5. Finish with kasuri methi before serving.

Rich, balanced, and unapologetically Indian.
