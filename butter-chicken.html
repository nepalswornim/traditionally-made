Butter Chicken

Quick Visual

Ingredients (Serves 3–4)

  • 500g chicken thigh
  • Butter
  • Tomato puree
  • Garam masala
  • Kasuri methi
  • Cream
  • Ginger & garlic

Method

  1. Marinate and grill chicken.
  2. Prepare tomato butter sauce.
  3. Add spices and cream.
  4. Finish with kasuri methi.

Rich, balanced, and unapologetically Indian.
